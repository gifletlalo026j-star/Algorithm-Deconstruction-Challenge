# Reflection Questions

## 1. How did the AI’s explanation change your understanding of the algorithm?

The AI explained the algorithm in simple language and broke it into smaller steps. This helped me understand how the algorithm calculates a score for each task and then sorts the tasks from highest to lowest priority.

---

## 2. What aspects were still difficult to understand after AI explanation?

At first, it was difficult to understand how the different scores were combined to create the final priority score. After reviewing the explanation and the code, it became much clearer.

---

## 3. How would you explain this algorithm to another junior developer?

I would explain that the algorithm gives every task points based on its priority, due date, status, tags, and recent updates. After calculating the total points, it sorts the tasks so that the most important task appears first.

---

## 4. Did you test this understanding against AI?

Yes. I asked AI to explain the algorithm in simple English and compared the explanation with the source code. This helped confirm that I understood how the algorithm works.

---

## 5. How might you improve the algorithm based on your understanding?

I would improve the algorithm by allowing users to customize the scoring system. Different users or companies may have different priorities, so making the score values configurable would make the algorithm more flexible.
